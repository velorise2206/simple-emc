## Overview

**Velorise EMC** reimagines classic alchemical equivalence into a modern, deeply integrated progression system. Instead of simply being an item-duplicating shortcut, Velorise EMC weaves transmutation, **World Tier** milestones, **Wireless Cores**, **Arcane Weapons**, world-generated **Resonance Spires**, and an endgame boss encounter into one cohesive survival adventure.

```text
Explore and fight
→ unlock World Tiers (I–IV)
→ reveal Resonant Ores
→ craft Wireless Cores & Alchemical Anvils
→ upgrade equipment, utilities & machines
→ conquer Resonance Spires & The Last Echo endgame
```

---

## Key Features

### ⚖️ Personal EMC Fund & Transmutation
- **Transmutation Table & Portable Tablets:** Burn surplus materials into your personal EMC fund, learn their blueprint once, and materialize items on demand.
- **Energy Condensers & Collectors:** Automate continuous resource generation and item transmutation.
- **Philosopher's Stone:** Transmute blocks directly in the world and craft high-density alchemical fuels.

### 🌟 World Tier Progression & Resonant Ores
- Advance through **World Tiers I–IV** via the **World Progress** screen by conquering Overworld, Nether, End, and Deep World challenges.
- Advancing tiers reveals hidden **Resonant Ores**, unlocks higher-tier recipes, and scales **World Threat** dynamically for single-player and multiplayer.

### 🔮 Wireless Cores & Universal Upgrades
- Craft **Wireless Cores I–IV** and **Alchemical Anvils** to install core upgrades into tools, armor, utilities, and machines.
- Increase power, range, capacity, and throughput while keeping EMC action costs balanced.

### ⚔️ Resonant Equipment & Arcane Weapons
- Forge **Resonant Tools & Armor** with tier-scaled stats and EMC-powered abilities.
- Wield **Arcane Weapons** with unique combat mechanics and **Echobound** endgame gear.

### 🏛️ Resonance Spires & The Last Echo
- Discover world-generated **Resonance Spires** with risk-reward challenges.
- Craft the **Eye of the Last Echo** after World Tier IV to locate the **Citadel**, defeat the **Echobound Dragon**, and unlock endgame **Echobound** equipment.

## Quick Start

1. Craft a **Philosopher's Stone** and a **Transmutation Table**.
2. Burn items with EMC values and learn useful materials.
3. Open **World Progress** from the Transmutation interface and complete the Overworld Hunt to unlock **World Tier I**.
4. Mine **Verdant Resonance**, craft **Wireless Core I** and **Alchemical Anvil I**, and begin upgrading your gear and machines.

For detailed guides on every system, see the **[Official Wiki](https://github.com/velorise2206/simple-emc/wiki)**.
